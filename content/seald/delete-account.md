You can ask for your seald account to be deleted from this page. There is no web form.

## 1. How to request deletion

**Email [jesse@jesseowen.xyz](mailto:jesse@jesseowen.xyz) from the Google account you used to sign in, with the subject "Delete my seald account".**

1. Use the Google account you used to sign in to seald.
2. Email [jesse@jesseowen.xyz](mailto:jesse@jesseowen.xyz).
3. Set the subject to **Delete my seald account**.

That email is the request. A short message is enough.

## 2. What is deleted

When the request is done, we delete:

- The Google-linked seald account
- The cloud collection synced to it

## 3. What is kept

Nothing on the server after that request is done.

## 4. Data on your phone

Local data on the phone stays until you sign out or uninstall. We cannot wipe a device from email.
