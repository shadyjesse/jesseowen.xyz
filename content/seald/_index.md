**seald** is an Android app for tracking sealed trading-card-game products (ETBs, booster boxes, bundles, tins and cases) for Pokémon and Magic: The Gathering.

- Scan the retail barcode to add a product
- Your collection is stored on your device and works offline
- See market value, and how it has moved over the timeframe you pick
- Search the seald sealed catalog by name
- Optional Google Sign-In for free cloud sync, with your collection also available on the web at [seald.xyz](https://seald.xyz)

Guest mode works without an account.
